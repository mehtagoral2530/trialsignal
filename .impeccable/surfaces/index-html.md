# Surface brief: TrialSignal site (index.html, all views)

Scope: the whole site, which is Studies (home), the study page, News, About, and a Saved drawer.

Visitor mode: Read. The site serves a layperson who wants to understand one study.

Audience and job:
- **Audience:** someone with no research background, who heard about a treatment.
- **Job:** find out what was tested, what was found, whether it was safe, and how sure we can be, in plain English.

Constraints:
- Light and calm.
- Low cognitive load.
- Not playful.
- No invented claims.
- The IRB verification statement stays visible.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Answer first, details on request. Every study is reframed as an everyday question, with a one-paragraph plain answer and a single "how sure" label. This refuses the research-database layout of tables, codes and phase jargon.

OWN-WORLD:
- **Ground and surfaces:** airy sky-white ground (#F5F8FC), white rounded surfaces with soft shadows.
- **Ink:** soft navy (#1F2B38).
- **Accent:** one friendly blue (#2F5BD3) for actions only.
- **Verdict labels:** four soft-tinted labels for "how sure": Proven and approved (green), Shown to work (teal), Promising, not checked yet (amber), Results not in yet (slate).
- **Type and icons:** Figtree; line icons in one stroke weight.

STORY: The visitor picks a topic or searches, opens a study phrased as a question, reads the short answer and its label, then opens one "Want to know more?" item at a time.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- **Hero:** a centered headline, "Understand a medical study in plain English", then a one-line explanation.
- **Search:** a wide rounded search bar with a blue Search button.
- **Topics:** six topic chips with icons.
- **Below:** "Studies people ask about" as question cards with verdict labels.

FORM: the plain-language health guide (patient leaflet and Q&A). This is a user-requested replacement of the evidence-certainty world.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
